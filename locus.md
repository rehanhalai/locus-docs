# Locus — Surveillance Forensic Suite

**Project:** Locus  
**PS ID:** 26150 (SIH 2026) | **Theme:** Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics  

---

## Overview

**Locus** is a unified, vendor-agnostic desktop forensic analysis platform for surveillance evidence acquisition, proprietary filesystem/video parsing, deleted footage recovery, multi-camera timeline synchronization, cryptographic validation, AI analytics, and court-ready legal reporting across major DVR/NVR OEMs (Dahua, CP Plus, Honeywell, TP-Link, Godrej, Uniview, HIKVISION, Matrix).
